## 🕯️ Project 1: Ofrenda // Sovereign (Option B)

### Vision
*An air-gapped, sovereign Día de los Muertos memory altar and ancestral chronicler.*

### Core Philosophy
Grief, family history, and remembrance of loved ones are deeply sacred. Uploading ancestor photographs, letters, and voice memos to corporate cloud AI vendors allows proprietary platforms to exploit and monetize intimate human memories. **Ofrenda // Sovereign** runs 100% locally on device storage with Google Gemma 2, guaranteeing complete sovereignty and privacy.

### Key Capabilities
1. **Interactive Multi-Tiered Altar (Canvas / WebGL):**
   * Traditional 3-tiered or 7-tiered altar with flickering beeswax candles, marigold (*cempasúchil*) petal physics, and drifting copal incense smoke.
2. **Digital Offerings Vault:**
   * Place personal artifacts onto the altar: ancestor photos, family recipes, journal entries, or audio snippets.
3. **Local Commemorative Chronicler:**
   * Local **Google Gemma 2** synthesizes reverent memorial chronicles and traditional *calaveritas literarias* (satirical/affectionate Day of the Dead poems).
4. **Self-Contained Heirloom Export:**
   * Compiles memories into an offline, single-file HTML or PDF archive that requires zero cloud accounts and remains readable decades into the future.

## 📻 Project 2: Ghostwire // 1999 (Option C)

### Vision
*A haunted retro CRT transceiver channeling dead protocols, forgotten BBS nodes, and early web folklore.*

### Core Philosophy
The early web was quirky, decentralized, and human. As ancient protocols and legacy architectures fade, their folklore disappears. **Ghostwire // 1999** revives early net history in a tactile, atmospheric terminal environment.

### Key Capabilities
1. **Authentic Phosphorus CRT Simulation:**
   * Amber/green monochrome CRT display with procedural scanline roll, tube distortion, blooming phosphor text, and chromatic aberration.
2. **Procedural Web Audio Engine:**
   * Realistic 56k dial-up modem handshake synthesis, magnetic CRT tube hum, and mechanical buckling-spring keyboard clatter.
3. **Dead Protocol & Language Séance:**
   * Gemma 2 roleplays as a 1990s BBS sysadmin / ghost operator channeling ancient systems: COBOL, Turbo Pascal, Commodore 64 BASIC, GeoCities sites, and Usenet newsgroups.
4. **Interactive Dial-Up Terminal:**
   * Connect to simulated "nodes" across early computing history to recover lost knowledge.
